## Rules

- Every entry must have an owner and a deadline.
- Closing an entry requires either a fix commit (link the SHA) or a ground-4 rule amendment.
- The Novice master (maintainer) scans this file monthly. Items past deadline without a fix get escalated back to the top of the task queue.
- If three entries share the same `AGENTS.md` section, that section needs amending — not more deferrals.
